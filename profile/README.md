Founded on July 3, 2025, Neawe Forge is a small game and app studio that started with one person. We make fun and just as educational games for multiple platforms, and we'd rather put out a few valuable products than many we're not proud of.

### 🔨 Words & Hammers

> Find the words among the letters, break the useless ones, and go step by step for your best score.

Every player gets the same letters. Find the words you're after, use hammers to break the useless letters and move on — every hammer costs points while every word earns them, so the smartest strategy wins.
